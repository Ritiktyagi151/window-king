The uPVC slide and folding door system consists of multiple panels that can be folded and stacked to one or both sides of the opening, providing a flexible and space-saving solution for large openings. The doors can be customized to fit any size or shape of the opening and are available in a range of colors and finishes to match the style of the building.
Some of the key features and benefits of uPVC slide and folding doors include:
Energy efficiency: uPVC is an excellent insulator, which makes it ideal for use in doors and windows. uPVC slide and folding doors are energy-efficient and can help to reduce heating and cooling costs by keeping the interior of the building at a consistent temperature.
Durability: uPVC is a strong and durable material that can withstand harsh weather conditions, making it ideal for use in doors and windows.
Low maintenance: uPVC slide and folding doors are low maintenance and do not require painting or staining. They can be easily cleaned with soap and water.
Space-saving: The slide and fold design of uPVC doors allows for a wide opening and seamless transition between indoor and outdoor spaces, making them an ideal solution for buildings with limited space.
Aesthetics: uPVC slide and folding doors have a sleek and modern design that can enhance the look of any building. They are available in a range of colors and finishes to match the style of the building.
Overall, uPVC slide and folding doors are a practical and stylish door option that provides a range of benefits for both residential and commercial buildings. They are energy-efficient, durable, low-maintenance, and provide a space-saving solution for large openings, making them a popular choice for homeowners and businesses alike
